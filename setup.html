Power on

Plug it in. Wait ten seconds.

Connect CLIMAS to any USB power source: a phone charger, a laptop port, or a power bank. The OLED screen runs through the startup animation, then shows CLIMAS SETUP.

That screen means the device is ready and waiting. Nothing else to press at this stage.

Connect
On your phone or laptop

Open WiFi settings and join CLIMAS-Setup.

Go to your phone's WiFi settings, the same place you connect to home or café networks. You'll see a network named CLIMAS-Setup. Tap it. No password needed.

This is a short-range network created by the device itself just for this step. It doesn't have internet access, that's normal and expected.

Setup page
Automatic

A setup page opens on its own.

Most phones detect the network and open a setup page automatically, the same way a hotel WiFi shows a login screen when you connect. You don't need to open a browser or type anything.

If it doesn't appear after a few seconds, open any browser and go to 192.168.4.1, the device's address during setup. The page loads immediately.

Your details
Four fields

Enter your WiFi and your location.

The setup page has four fields. Your home WiFi name, selected from a dropdown of nearby networks. Your password. Your city name, typed exactly as you would in Google Maps. And your two-letter country code.

The country code is just two letters: GH Ghana   NG Nigeria   GB UK   US USA. The setup page has a row of tappable codes to make this easy, just tap yours and it fills in automatically.

Tap Save & Connect. The page confirms, the device restarts.

CLIMAS connects and starts monitoring.

CLIMAS restarts, connects to your WiFi, and begins working immediately. Reconnect your phone to your normal network, you're done.

The screen rotates every few seconds between three views: indoor temperature and humidity; outdoor weather for the city you entered; and the live gas reading. Every reading is specific to your location.

To change your city or WiFi at any time, hold the button for three seconds. CLIMAS resets and goes back to step one.

Common questions

Type it in plain English without accent marks, Abidjan not Abidján, Sao Paulo not São Paulo. You can check that your city name works at openweathermap.org before setup.
